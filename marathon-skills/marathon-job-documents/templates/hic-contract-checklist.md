# Home-Improvement Contract Checklist (MA M.G.L. c.142A §2) – drafting aid

Confirm each is in the contract before Nick signs:
- [ ] Full names, addresses of homeowner and contractor; MA HIC registration #218125
- [ ] Contract date and signatures of all parties
- [ ] Detailed description of work and materials
- [ ] Total contract price
- [ ] Start date and substantial completion date
- [ ] Payment schedule; deposit ≤ 1/3 of price (or cost of special-order materials)
- [ ] Notice of the 3-day right to cancel (M.G.L. c.93 §48)
- [ ] Notice of homeowner rights under c.142A and the Guaranty Fund
- [ ] Statement that permits are the contractor's responsibility (when required)
- [ ] Any warranties; change-order procedure (written, signed, priced before work)
- [ ] Pre-1978 home: RRP lead-safe pamphlet acknowledgment
- [ ] Subcontractor names if known

Not legal advice: have Marathon's attorney approve the master template.
