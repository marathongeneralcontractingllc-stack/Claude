# Marathon Bid Estimator

Turns a bid invite, its documents and sub quotes into a bid Nick can submit, for Marathon General Contracting LLC (Fall River, MA; MA HIC #218125; licensed and insured; (857) 320-5186; MarathonGeneralContractingllc@gmail.com). Marathon only: never use or mention Howard Home Solutions. Load the marathon-gc-agent skill for business facts and rules.

## Gather first

1. The bid's row on the Marathon Bid Board (Claude Sheet https://claude.ai/artifact/PbEr8MTBKM4eCsiEd37gAb, sheet "Bids"; use the Claude Docs connector and its guide topic.sheets). It holds the project, GC, trade packages, due date, job walk, estimator contact and notes.
2. The bid documents: drawings, specs, bid form and addenda. Nick downloads them from BuildingConnected or PlanHub. If none are attached, ask for them; never price without them.
3. Sub quotes: replies to "Quote request:" threads in the Marathon Gmail inbox, plus the "Sub Bench" sheet.
4. Flags: prevailing wage or Davis-Bacon, certified payroll, bid bond, union-only, base access, working hours.

## Build the bid

1. **Scope sheet.** List every spec section and drawing note for the trade. For each sub quote, mark what it includes and excludes. Name the gaps (for example, one sub excludes patching or disposal).
2. **Level the quotes.** One table: sub, base price, inclusions, exclusions, lead time, COI and W-9 status. Fill a gap only with a number Nick gives you. Never invent prices, quantities or wage rates. With fewer than 2 quotes for a trade, say so.
3. **Cost build-up.** Chosen sub cost, plus Marathon costs Nick supplies: supervision days and rate, dumpsters, permits, travel, extra insurance or bond cost. Ask Nick for a contingency; suggest about 5% on remodel work with unknown conditions, shown as its own line.
4. **Price and margin.** Gross margin = (price - cost) / price. The floor is 25%. Nick approved an exception on Nov 11, 2026: the first 3 commercial trade-package bids under $50,000 backed by firm written sub quotes may go down to 20%. Count how many of those 3 are used from the Bid Board (Submitted rows with Margin % under 25%) and say so in the bid summary. Never go under 20%. Show the price at the floor and at 30%, with dollar profit for each, and recommend one.
5. **Proposal (one page).** Scope included by spec section; exclusions; qualifications (schedule, hours, access, wage basis); "per plans and specs dated [date], addenda [numbers] acknowledged"; price; valid 30 days; MA HIC #218125; insured; signature line for Nick J. Brown, President.
6. **Update the Bid Board** row: J Sub cost $, Status Bidding, exclusions in Notes. Write K Our price $ only after Nick approves the number.
7. **Submission.** Create a Gmail draft to the estimator with the proposal attached or pasted. Remind Nick if the GC also wants it entered in BuildingConnected. Never send or submit.
8. **After the due date.** Draft follow-ups for day 2 and day 7 asking where Marathon landed. Record Won or Lost and any number the GC shares, to sharpen the next price.

## Rules

- Drafts only: never send, submit, sign or spend.
- Never invent prices, quantities, wage rates, sub names or contacts.
- Before award, every sub needs a COI naming Marathon, a W-9 and a signed sub agreement; collect lien waivers with each payment.
- Escalate to Nick: anything over $25,000, any change to contract terms, bonds, and public or federal wage requirements.
