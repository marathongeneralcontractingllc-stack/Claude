## 4. The revenue engine (how marketing turns into money)

Revenue = leads × contact rate × estimate rate × close rate × average ticket × repeat and referral. Work every lever, in this order of payoff:

**Lever 1: Speed to lead.** The first contractor to respond usually wins. Target: every lead contacted within 5 minutes during business hours, 15 minutes at most. Any lead still New after 15 minutes is the top priority of the day. Missed calls get a text back within 5 minutes.

**Lever 2: Estimates that close.**
- Book the site visit on the first call; offer two time slots ("Tuesday at 4 or Wednesday at 9?").
- Send the written quote within 24 hours of the visit; never longer than 48.
- Quote **good / better / best** on every construction job (for example: standard rebuild, upgraded materials, premium with extras). Most people pick the middle, which raises the average ticket. Never present only one price.
- Give a real reason to decide now: the next open start date, a materials price hold until a stated date, or the snow-season schedule. Never fake scarcity.
- Follow up on unsigned quotes on day 2, day 5 and day 10, then monthly. Each follow-up adds something useful (a photo of similar work, an answer to a likely question, a schedule update).
- Track why quotes are lost (price, timing, went with someone else, no response) and adjust.

**Lever 3: Reviews (the #1 local ranking factor).** Ask every happy customer for a Google review at the moment of delight: after the first storm is cleared (send a photo of their clean driveway with the ask), and at the final walk-through of every job. Review link: https://g.page/r/Cfw0uEyew95AEBM/review. Log the date in the sheet's "Review asked" column. Reply to every review within 24 hours, by name, specific and thankful. Goal: 25 Google reviews by March 1, 2027. Never offer payment or discounts for reviews, never write reviews, never review-gate (ask everyone).

**Lever 4: Referrals and street density (approved rewards).**
- **Snow referral: $50 credit** to the referring customer for every referred neighbor who signs a seasonal plan (stacks with the 3-house neighbor deal).
- **Construction referral: $100** to the referrer for every referred construction job that books and pays its deposit.
- Log who referred whom in the sheet's "Referred by" column and pay rewards after the referred customer pays.
- Within 48 hours of each snow signup, leave door hangers on the 10 nearest houses: "We're already clearing driveways on your street this winter. Sign up and save." Never reveal the customer's address or name. Dense streets mean more stops per hour and more profit per storm.
- Ask every completed construction customer: "Who else do you know who's been putting off a project? If they book, we'll send you $100."

**Lever 5: Upsell and cross-sell ladder.** At every touchpoint, offer the next logical add-on, once, without pressure:
- Snow signup: walkway and steps, ice melt, sidewalk and hydrant.
- Snow customer in Jan–Mar: spring deck, patio or interior project ("Snow customers get first pick of spring start dates").
- Demolition: dumpster rental. Dumpster rental: cleanout labor or demo.
- Every finished construction job: snow plan for next winter.

**Lever 6: Repeat and renewal (approved).** Snow is recurring revenue. **Every Sept 1, offer every past snow customer next season at last season's price if they renew by Oct 1**, with card-on-file or paid in full. Every quarter, reactivate Lost leads and old unsigned quotes with a short, fresh message tied to the season.

**Lever 7: High-value accounts.** One landlord, property manager, HOA, church or small business can equal 5–20 homeowners. Every week, list three local property managers, landlords or small commercial lots worth approaching, and draft a short intro for Nicholas. Watch Leadfeeder for businesses visiting the site and draft follow-ups. Pitch a single annual agreement for snow plus repairs.

**Lever 8: Paid ads, only with guardrails.** Organic and referrals first. Paid ads only with Nicholas's approval of the budget, and only with these rules:
