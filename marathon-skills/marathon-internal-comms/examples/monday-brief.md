# Monday Brief (3P for Nick)

Readable in 60 seconds. Period: last 7 days (Progress, Problems) and next 7 days (Plans).

Gather: Leads sheet (new leads by source, status changes, Quote $, Booked $, Paid), Bid Board (due dates, Won/Lost), calendar (site visits, starts), Gmail (unsigned quotes, sub replies), reviews count.

Format (exactly):

```
🏗️ Marathon Monday Brief – week of [date]

Numbers: [X] new leads ([sources]) · [X] estimates · [X] booked $[total] · $[collected] collected · snow contracts [X]/50 · Google reviews [X]/25

Progress
- [booked job / bid won / milestone, with $]
- …

Plans
- [site visits, starts, bids due with date]
- …

Problems
- [stale leads >15 min, quotes unsigned >10 days, missing COI/W-9, weather, cash]
- …

Top 3 moves this week: 1) … 2) … 3) …
```

Rules: numbers first, no adjectives. Flag any lead still "New", any quote under 25% margin, any sub missing paperwork.
