# Monthly Customer Newsletter

Audience: past and current customers and leads. Email + repurposed as one Facebook post. Under 250 words.

Sections:
1. **Headline offer** for the month from the campaign calendar (marathon-gc-agent §6), one CTA with `?src=` link.
2. **Recent work**: 1–2 projects, before/after photo, one sentence each, no addresses.
3. **Tip of the month** (storm prep, sidewalk/hydrant rules, why winter is good for interior remodels, deck care…).
4. **Referral reminder**: $50 snow credit / $100 construction thank-you.
5. Footer: brand footer from marathon-brand-guidelines and an unsubscribe line.

Never invent testimonials; only quote real reviews with permission.
