# Customer Job-Status Update

Send at: start date confirmed, start of each week on site, any change order, any delay, substantial completion, final walk-through.

Format (text or email, under 120 words):

```
Hi [first name], quick update on your [project]:

Done: [what got finished]
Next: [what happens next, with day]
Need from you: [selection / access / decision by date] (or "Nothing right now")
[If change order: "Change order #[n] for [item], $[amount], is attached for your signature. We won't start that work until it's signed."]

Questions? Text me at (857) 320-5186.
– Nick, Marathon GC
```

Rules: never promise a date you can't keep; delays get the reason and the new date. Change orders are always written and signed before the work. At the final walk-through, add the review ask from marathon-gc-agent §7 and the referral ask.
