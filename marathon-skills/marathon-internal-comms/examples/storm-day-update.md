# Storm-Day Ops Update (Marathon Snow & Ice)

Three messages per storm. Trigger: 2 in. Commitments: driveways within 6 hrs after snowfall stops (12 hrs for 12 in+); sidewalks/hydrants within 5 daylight hours (Fall River City Code § 66-32).

1. **Pre-storm (customer text, 12–24 hrs before):**
   "Marathon Snow & Ice: [X–Y] in expected [day]. We're on it. Please keep cars off the end of the driveway if you can. Clearing starts once snow slows; done within 6 hours after it stops."
2. **Crew route (internal):** storm total forecast, crew assignments by street cluster (route order), ice-melt add-on list, hydrant/sidewalk list, start time, check-in times.
3. **Cleared (customer text + photo, no house number visible):**
   "Your driveway's clear. [Walkway/ice melt done.] Thanks for being a Marathon customer!" First storm of the season: add the review ask.
4. **Public posts:** same-day "Crews are out" and next-day cleared-driveway post (marathon-gc-agent §6). No addresses.

Track per-visit charges for 2–6 / 6–12 / 12 in+ customers and seasonal snowfall toward the 60 in cap.
