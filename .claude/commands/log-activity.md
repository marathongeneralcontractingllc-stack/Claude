Add one row to the Notion **Activity Log** database (inside Marathon Brain) for: $ARGUMENTS

Fields:
- **What happened**: one plain sentence.
- **Who**: "Claude - chat", unless a scheduled lane (Bid Desk, Sub Bench, Back Office, CEO Brief, Morning Desk, Evening Desk) did the work.
- **When**: now, as a datetime in America/New_York.
- **System**: every system touched (Gmail, HighLevel, Notion, Zapier, HubSpot, Calendar, Meta, BuildingConnected, Other).
- **Details**: what changed, where, and what was *not* done (be exact: drafted, not sent).
- **Needs**: Nick, Grok, Claude or Nothing.
- **Link**: the main artifact, PR or page, if there is one.

Never put tokens, passwords or full card or bank numbers in the row.
